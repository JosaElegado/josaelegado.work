Problems worth solving

Four, regardless of industry

  1. I

    Making complex things easier to understand.

    Whether it’s solar energy, government programs, or emerging technologies, I enjoy turning complexity into something people can confidently act on.

  2. II

    Helping good ideas reach the people who need them.

    Too many promising ideas never make it beyond the laboratory, the whiteboard, or the pitch deck. I’m interested in closing that gap.

  3. III

    Creating opportunities for the next generation.

    Whether it’s mentoring young founders, supporting inventors, or encouraging women in entrepreneurship, I believe progress grows when more people are invited to participate.

  4. IV

    Leaving every system a little better than I found it.

    Communities, organizations, relationships, and the environment all thrive when we contribute more than we consume.
