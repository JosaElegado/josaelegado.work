Problems worth solving

Six, regardless of industry

The kinds of challenges I keep coming back to, regardless of the industry.

  1. Making complex things easier to understand.

    Whether it’s solar energy, government programs, or emerging technologies, I enjoy turning complexity into something people can confidently act on.

  2. Helping good ideas reach the people who need them.

    Too many promising ideas never make it beyond the laboratory, the whiteboard, or the pitch deck. I’m interested in closing that gap.

  3. Designing systems that work for people — not just on paper.

    Technology, policies, and businesses only succeed when they fit naturally into people’s lives.

  4. Building trust before asking for change.

    Real adoption starts with listening. People rarely resist change — they resist being misunderstood or left behind.

  5. Creating opportunities for the next generation.

    Whether it’s mentoring young founders, supporting inventors, or encouraging women in entrepreneurship, I believe progress grows when more people are invited to participate.

  6. Leaving every system a little better than I found it.

    Communities, organizations, relationships, and the environment all thrive when we contribute more than we consume.
